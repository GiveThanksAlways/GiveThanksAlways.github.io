TALK · PLANETNIX 2026

Running tinygrad on the NVIDIA Jetson: talking to the GPU without CUDA

From tinygrad's computation graph to PTX to a cubin, then straight to the GPU through raw ioctls (GPFIFO, QMDs, MMIO) instead of CUDA. Also: standing it up on NixOS/JetPack, a drone dead-reckoning demo (IMU → temporal CNN + EKF), benchmarks against PyTorch and TensorRT, and porting tinygrad's NV backend to Tegra.
